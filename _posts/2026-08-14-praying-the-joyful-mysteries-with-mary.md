The Joyful Mysteries carry a mood of wonder: a promise received, a journey made, a child welcomed, and a life quietly offered to God.

## Linger with the scene

You do not need to force a vivid picture. Read the mystery slowly, notice what draws your attention, and let one phrase stay with you through the decade.

## Pray with openness

Mary’s yes, her journey, and her careful remembering can make room for your own hopes and questions. Bring them honestly, then return to the prayer when your mind moves on.
