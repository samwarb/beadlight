Sunday can carry a different pace. Even a few unhurried minutes can help you notice what the week has made difficult to hear.

## Make room

Set the phone aside, take a comfortable seat, and let the first prayer be a genuine arrival. You are not trying to perform calm; you are simply making space for it.

## Stay with the beads

If the day feels full, allow the Rosary to be a gentle boundary around this time. You can finish with one simple prayer of thanks for being here.
