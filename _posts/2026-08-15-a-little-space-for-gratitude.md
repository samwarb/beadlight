Gratitude does not require a perfect day. Sometimes it begins with one small gift you almost overlooked: a person, a breath, a patch of light, or the chance to start again.

## Name what is here

As you move through the beads, quietly name one thing you receive with thanks. Let the list stay small enough to feel real.

## Let thanks widen

Gratitude is not pretending that difficulty is absent. It is allowing goodness to sit beside it. The Rosary gives both a place to be held.
