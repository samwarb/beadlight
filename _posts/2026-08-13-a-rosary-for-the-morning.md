Morning prayer does not need to be long to be real. Before messages, plans, and noise begin, let the beads give you a small, steady place to start.

## Begin with one intention

Choose one person, hope, or worry to hold before God. You do not need to solve it while you pray. Simply let it accompany you through the mysteries.

## Keep it gentle

If the morning is rushed, pray one decade and return later. A faithful beginning can be simple: a quiet breath, the next bead, and the willingness to begin.
