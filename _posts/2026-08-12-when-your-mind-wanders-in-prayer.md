A wandering mind does not mean you have failed at prayer. Attention naturally moves; the quiet work is noticing and returning.

## Return without worry

When you realise you are somewhere else, do not scold yourself or start over. Let the next bead become a small doorway back to the prayer. You can return as many times as you need.

## Three simple helps

- Slow your breathing before the next prayer.
- Hold one intention gently, rather than trying to think about everything.
- Let the words be enough, even when your feelings are quiet.

The Rosary is a steady path, not a test of perfect concentration. Each return is a little act of trust.

Beadlight keeps the sequence close at hand, so you can spend less energy remembering what comes next and more time being present.
