Stillness is often quieter than we expect. It may not feel like an empty mind; it may feel like noticing where you are and returning without blame.

## Let the next bead be enough

You do not have to hold the whole Rosary in your mind at once. Stay with the prayer in front of you, then let the next one arrive.

## Return kindly

Every distraction offers another chance to begin again. A slow breath, relaxed shoulders, and one familiar prayer can make the way back feel possible.

Peace can grow in the space between trying to be perfect and simply being present.
