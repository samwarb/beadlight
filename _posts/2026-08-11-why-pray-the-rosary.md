The Rosary is a simple way to stay with the life of Jesus. Its repeated prayers give the mind somewhere steady to return, while the mysteries lead us through moments of Christ’s life with Mary.

## A gentle rhythm

You do not need a perfect room or a perfectly quiet mind. Begin with one bead, one breath, and one intention. The rhythm of the prayers can make space for gratitude, sorrow, hope, and quiet attention.

The Rosary is not about rushing to finish. It is an invitation to remain present.

## Begin simply

Choose a time that you can keep, hold the beads, and let the next prayer be enough. If your attention wanders, return gently without starting again.
